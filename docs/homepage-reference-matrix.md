# Bytes Homepage — Reference Matrix

**Method.** Every reference site was opened in a real Chromium browser (Playwright 1.61) at desktop 1440×900 and mobile 390×844, allowed to load fonts/media/WebGL (8s settle), consent overlays dismissed where present, then scrolled gradually top-to-bottom with viewport screenshots captured at 9 desktop / 5 mobile scroll states. Frames were then visually analyzed. Limitations are stated per site; observations are labeled **observed** (visible across frames) vs **inferred** (implied but not provable from stills — e.g., pointer-follow behavior, easing curves, audio).

Sites that are fully scroll-jacked single-viewport WebGL experiences (document height = 900px: **igloo.inc**, **flowty.co**) load and render live, but wheel input drives an internal scene rather than the document — captured states for these are more limited and flagged as such.

No assets, models, signatures, or imagery from any reference site will be reused. Behavior and art-direction study only.

## 1. vectrfl.com

- **Inspection status**: Direct live-browser scroll capture, **partially degraded on desktop**: frames 00–06 show only the site's preloader (pixel-arrow logo assembling from squares over dotted orbital ellipses) — the WebGL scene never painted at those scroll states in headless Chromium. Frames 07–08 show the loaded hero text without the 3D environment. **Mobile captures rendered the full experience**, so most interaction analysis comes from the mobile frames.
- **Observed interaction**: (mobile frames) Hero text above a light monochromatic isometric 3D city — white/ice-blue low-detail buildings, tiny figures, a single red route line threading between buildings as the only accent. Next frame: camera has moved to a different district (silo, sheds, warehouse rooftops, halftone ground patch, glowing blue beam) while a pinned numbered process list (01 Activation simplified / 02 Cleared to count / 03 Proven field match / 04 Seamless arrival) sits over the scene; the active step is enlarged with a white pill around its number. Later: the list advanced to step 04 and the scene has transformed into a giant extruded arrow glyph with floating diamond tiles — the environment morphs to match the step. Then conventional card/photo sections and a dark navy footer with oversized wordmark. Desktop frames 07–08 show hero type settling from a per-line 3D tilt. (Continuous camera glide between steps: inferred.)
- **What makes it effective**: One continuous environment carries the whole narrative — the world reorganizes itself around each process step, making an abstract service feel physical. Near-monochrome + one accent keeps a complex 3D scene legible and typography dominant. The pinned numbered list maps scroll progress to an explicit "where am I in the story."
- **Bytes adaptation**: Strongest template for the **Byte City hero** — isometric dark-navy city of capability structures, monochrome navy with ONE electric-blue data route threading between buildings. The pinned numbered-list pattern informs the Capability Story progress rail and the Process Network. Their assembling-from-squares preloader maps to a "bytes assembling" SystemLoader concept. Dotted trajectory arcs → data-path linework behind the hero headline.
- **Must NOT copy**: The white/ice-blue construction-city aesthetic and literal building types (silos, warehouses, hard-hat figures), the red route color, the pixel-arrow logo motif, the wordmark treatment, "SCROLL TO DISCOVER OUR PROCESS" verbatim. Bytes' city is data infrastructure, not a construction site.
- **Performance risk**: High — their own desktop WebGL failed to paint for ~7 consecutive capture states. Mitigate: low-poly primitives (no GLTF payloads), poster-first paint, copy in real DOM (as they do), no scroll-jacking of the document.
- **Mobile fallback**: Observed — vectr runs the same 3D scene on mobile. Bytes will NOT: poster + CSS parallax on mobile (safer, per brief).

## 4. igloo.inc

- **Inspection status**: Direct live-browser capture, **heavily limited as expected**: pageHeight=900 confirms a fully scroll-jacked single-viewport WebGL canvas, so the harness produced essentially one desktop + one mobile state.
